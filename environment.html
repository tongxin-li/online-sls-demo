A distribution feeder under changing power

The experiment regulates a constructed, balanced radial feeder with 24 buses. A fixed substation supplies homes, solar generation and batteries. The target is the nominal voltage profile before power disturbances.

Physical setting

Electrical bases12.47 kV line to line and 1 MVA
Lines23 branches with resistance 0.45 to 0.90 Ω and reactance 0.30 to 0.46 Ω
Nominal power70 kW per ordinary bus and 50 kW per battery bus, at power factor 0.95. Solar buses additionally generate 80 kW.
InvertersBuses 5, 7, 8, 12, 14, 18, 21 and 24. Each has a 120 kVA rating and a reactive command limit of ±70 kvar.
Prediction messagesA sparse controller receives its local prediction and at most three remote sources per inverter.
TimeOne second per sample with a two second inverter response time
vₜ = 1 − Rᵥpₜ − Xᵥqₜload + XᵥEqₜinv

Rᵥ and Xᵥ sum branch impedances along shared substation paths. E places inverter injections at their buses. The reference v̄ uses nominal power and zero inverter reactive injection, ranging from 0.98738 to 1 pu.

Disturbances and observations

Background net power follows a correlated process with a 6 kW scale. The forecast presets provide reliable, uniformly corrupted or mixed quality predictions of the resulting voltage forcing. Each prediction is fixed when issued, and clicked events are absent from it. Compared controllers receive identical power disturbances and issued forecasts.

Clicking a home adds demand at power factor 0.95. Clicking solar reduces generation. Clicking a battery increases charging. Requests of 50, 100 or 150 kW decay over several seconds. Repeated requests add before equipment limits apply. Solar loss cannot exceed available generation, and battery charging is capped at 90 kW. The substation remains fixed.

Continuous mode is selected by default. Click a bus to add one request per physical sample until the same bus is clicked again, Stop attack is pressed or playback is paused. In Pulse mode, click once for one request or hold a bus or Attack to repeat until release.

Orange lightning badges show the actual applied disturbance in kW after equipment limits. Pending means a request is queued for the next sample. Chart badges mark Attack or Attacks, and shaded intervals show when disturbances occur.

Displayed quantities

cₜ = 35‖vₜ − v̄‖² + 0.08‖uₜ‖²

Voltage is displayed in pu and inverter injection in kvar. Cost uses the bounded reactive command u on the 1 MVA base. The live cumulative curve sums executed stage costs. A completed benchmark additionally includes the terminal voltage error penalty. A disturbance changes voltage at the next sample, and its voltage error is charged at that sample.

The 0.95 to 1.05 pu band is a reference operating band, not an enforced constraint. Voltage and cost are never clipped. Playback defaults to sixteen samples per second, with options of two, four, eight, sixteen and thirty two. Linear interpolation smooths the visual motion between samples. Playback speed changes viewing pace without changing the physical sample duration of one second.

The prediction matrix records actual connectivity updates in its history slider and recent event cards. Added links appear green and removed links amber, with a brief flash when an update arrives. Coefficients can change while connectivity stays stable. Discovery history remains available during deployment, when the selected prediction links stay fixed.

LinDistFlow neglects line losses and assumes small voltage deviations. This experiment omits unbalanced phases, thermal line limits, protection, frequency dynamics and battery energy limits. It is a constructed environment rather than a measured utility case.
