Forecasting changing feeder demand

A choice of constructed radial feeders supplies homes, solar generation and batteries. The default experiment asks whether useful advance forecasts can keep voltage closer to its nominal profile when some forecast sources are corrupted. It is an illustrative physical scenario, not measured utility data or a numerical reproduction of the paper.

Network sizes

The Network menu starts a fresh trial on the chosen feeder. Every size uses the same physical equations, equipment ratings, cost weights and learning rules. Counts and results belong to the selected network. Absolute cost sums over its buses and inverters, so costs from different sizes are not directly comparable.

Forecasted operating cycle

Fresh trials default to Strong stress. A coordinated surge repeats every 30 seconds and adds up to 120 kW at each aggregate household bus, removes up to 60 kW of solar generation and adds up to 60 kW of battery charging. Each surge rises over two seconds, holds for four seconds and falls over two seconds. The second radial branch starts four seconds later. The larger feeder repeats these branch delays. This constructed stress profile creates a larger changing flow through the existing lines. It does not alter their impedance or any controller.

Standard retains the original 60 second profiles. Household demand varies by ±35 kW, solar output falls by 0 to 30 kW and battery power varies by ±15 kW. Both settings add the unchanged correlated background power with a 6 kW scale. These are synthetic aggregate excursions, not measured household traces. Selecting another stress level starts a new trial. Previously saved trials retain their recorded environment.

Existing equipment bounds apply after the schedule and background are combined. The displayed scheduled kW describe this known component. Actual power also includes the background and any manual attacks. Every controller faces exactly the same realized power.

These profiles enter forecasts before they occur. A five sample preview includes the current action. The forcing for target action t is sealed at max(0, t − 4) and observed at state t + 1. Previously issued forecasts never change. Only observations already revealed may enter learning. The preview is a declared information assumption, not an implemented commercial forecasting service.

Source reliability

The default assumes reliable inverter forecast sources and corrupted household estimates. Inverter sources initially have low error and household sources have high error. Their counts are 3 quiet and 4 noisy for the toy, 8 and 15 for the reference, and 16 and 30 for the larger network. These are synthetic quality classes rather than measured sensor accuracies, and their labels are not supplied to the learner.

A source message estimates voltage forcing at its bus, which depends on power throughout the feeder. It is not simply a forecast of that bus’s own load. Independent Gaussian errors have standard deviation β times a fixed feeder forcing scale. The multiplier is 0.05 for quiet sources and 4 for noisy sources under Standard stress. Strong doubles only the noisy multiplier to 8, including sources corrupted by a click. The default β remains 4. The physical forcing scale remains based on the original 6 kW background and does not grow with the scheduled profile. No controller gain, objective or actuator limit is retuned.

Strong changes physical signal amplitude and faulty source noise separately, so its signal to noise ratio differs from Standard. More forecastable variation can make advance action useful. More forecast corruption can favor feedback only. Neither the profile nor the cost display guarantees a particular ranking.

The stress characterization retains the original cycle, an exploratory sustained excursion and the selected short surge, including unfavorable outcomes. Short surges reduce persistent inverter saturation and expose transient anticipation more clearly. The same actuator limits still constrain every policy. The selected profile can be reproduced with the public benchmark script.

The main trial heading summarizes current β and the configured quiet/noisy source counts. It is not an error measurement, and the learner never receives these labels. The summary changes immediately when a source setting changes, while already issued messages stay sealed. Noise disabled at β 0 does not announce manual power attacks. Clicking a source in Forecast mode corrupts or repairs newly issued messages. Corruption persists until repaired. Marking every source noisy removes the reliable alternatives that communication selection needs. In fresh trials, responses and incoming links continue learning from revealed observations after source reliability changes. Archived finite comparisons keep their deployed links fixed.

Ongoing adaptive protocol

Fresh trials continuously update the full predictive state and input response matrices. Learning uses completed response windows only, with five samples of preview, a window of 60 completed rows and 30 completed rows before the first update at action 39. Every later action updates the response. Current incoming prediction links are reconsidered every 30 updates. Voltage, time and every accumulated stage cost remain continuous.

Newly issued forecasts retain their admitted links and full response matrices until their finite memory expires. A removed link stops sending new forecasts immediately. Its previously received values can still affect the command for a few samples. The interface separates current incoming links from these retiring cached contributions.

The sparse incoming budgets remain 6, 24 and 48 remote links for the three network sizes. Full response learning uses a central collection of revealed forecast and forcing pairs, and broadcasts new response parameters. That learning traffic is reported separately. Sparse actuator links alone are not a claim of lower total communication.

Fixed sparse retains its initial support and response. Dense prediction retains its full support and response. Feedback only uses the same stabilizing feedback without predictions. All four receive identical physical disturbances and use the same objective and limits. Neither startup costs nor unfavorable outcomes are removed.

This is an experimental shared response specialization with online topology reselection. It is not a literal execution of the manuscript’s independent response row algorithm and does not inherit a convergence guarantee for the changing saturated controller. The implementation and validation details are in the response implementation record.

Saved earlier trials restore their original controller and all costs exactly. Start adaptive demo on a saved source correction trial starts a fresh paused trial with continuous response and topology learning. Restart demo and Network selection also start fresh paused trials. There is one public demo and no version menu.

The following finite discovery and comparison lifecycle and recorded results belong to archived runs. They cannot supply performance percentages for the ongoing protocol. Playback pace depends on network size and rendering load while each physical sample always represents one second. Earlier slow 47 bus outcomes are retained in the linked engineering evidence. Retaining all samples through more than 1800 actions is finite evidence, not an indefinite memory guarantee. Exact validation and publication status remains in the repository progress record.

Lower error can favor dense prediction. The scenario does not establish an advantage for every reliability map or every added attack.

Unexpected power attacks

Manual power attacks are additional, unforecast stress. Homes increase demand, solar generation decreases and batteries increase charging. Each 50, 100 or 150 kW request decays. Fresh trials select 150 kW; saved trials retain their selected strength. Solar loss is bounded by available generation and battery charging is capped at 90 kW. Continuous mode maintains the selected additional requested kW at each active bus by replacing that bus’s previous continuous component each sample. It does not silently multiply a 150 kW setting through overlapping requests. Applied power still respects equipment bounds. Stopping lets the final request decay with the existing equipment response. Pulse mode remains additive, including when held to repeat; its separate residuals can add to a continuous component. Saved trials recorded before this protocol retain their exact original stacked replay. Click again to stop one bus or use Stop all. Pause stops repeated requests, while existing disturbances remain in the plant.

All physical costs remain in the same trial. Switching interaction mode or reloading does not erase them. Large repeated attacks can dominate cumulative costs because every controller shares the same feedback. They do not promise to widen the advantage of prediction. The mixed trial label identifies these extra requests.

Experiment settings retain the original background only reference and the unannounced solar outage for reproducibility. The reference uses the original source map at bus multiples of three. The outage removes solar output during comparison actions 30 through 49, restores it at 50 and is absent from forecasts. Their complete results remain in the reference audit, outage audit and extended error audit.

Displayed quantities

cₜ = 35‖vₜ − v̄‖² + 0.08‖uₜ‖²

Voltage is in pu and reactive power in kvar. Cost uses the bounded reactive command on the 1 MVA base. The live chart sums executed stage costs. The numerical audit also reports the terminal Riccati penalty separately and includes it in total cost. A physical disturbance changes the next state, whose state cost appears at the following action.

Now and Peak show the largest voltage deviation from each bus’s nominal reference, in mpu where 1 mpu is 0.001 pu. Limited counts clipped inverter commands. The 0.95 to 1.05 pu band is illustrative and is not enforced. Voltage and cost are never clipped. A forecast error in mpu is a forcing error, not an actual voltage excursion.

The shared controller key appears before the plots. Its buttons change curve visibility only, not controllers, measurements or accumulated costs. Every method remains in the cost tables even when its curve is hidden.

Matrix entries show actual allowed prediction sources. Inspecting a cell reveals the last observed forecast error and the source’s contribution to an inverter command. Source, other messages and feedback sum before limiting. History changes only the displayed topology. Playback speed changes viewing pace rather than physical sample time.

LinDistFlow neglects line losses and assumes small voltage deviations. This environment omits unbalanced phases, thermal line limits, protection, frequency dynamics and battery energy limits. The feeder, reliability model and operating profiles are constructed. The paper uses a different coupled model, six discovery episodes and twelve remote sources per receiver.
